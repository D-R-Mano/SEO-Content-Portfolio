## The content problem

My work included articles about Roku streaming players and website copy for a US-facing audience. It required combining product information with search-focused writing: the content needed to address the reader's interest while remaining clear and relevant to the assigned topic.

## My contribution

I wrote keyword-led blog and website content, main-page copy, meta tags and descriptions, and short copy supporting visual marketing assets. I adapted wording for the intended audience and edited for grammar, consistency, and readability.

I also reviewed website performance indicators such as visits, time on site, and page views to inform content work. The underlying reports are no longer available, so this portfolio describes that responsibility without recreating analytics or attributing numerical results.

## The decisions the work required

**Stay close to the reader's question.** A product-related search needs a relevant explanation, not simply repeated product names. Keyword requirements informed the topic, while the body needed to give the reader useful information.

**Connect metadata to the page.** The title and description needed to reflect what the reader would actually find. A promise in search copy creates an expectation the article must satisfy.

**Adjust language for the market.** US-facing product copy required attention to phrasing and audience context, alongside technical understanding.
