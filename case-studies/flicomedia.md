# Flicomedia: discovery with an editorial voice

[Back to portfolio](../README.md)

**Website Content Writer · September 2017–March 2018**  
**Type:** Retrospective professional case study. Original articles and reporting are unavailable.

## The content setting

At Flicomedia, I worked on articles, blogs, app content, and social posts across travel, culture, entertainment, and events. Topics included Jallikattu and places to visit in Madurai.

The writing had to connect with reader interests while fitting the format in which people encountered it. An article allowed context and explanation; a social post needed a concise reason to engage.

## My contribution

I developed website and app content, used assigned keyword strategies, and contributed to Facebook and Instagram content and event coverage. The work involved adapting topics across articles, short write-ups, and social formats while maintaining an accessible editorial voice.

## What this experience taught me

**The topic is only the start.** A useful angle depends on what the reader wants to know about that topic.

**Discovery and readability need to work together.** Search-focused writing still needs a clear opening, useful organization, and language that sounds natural.

**Different channels need different copy.** An article introduction and a social caption should not be treated as interchangeable text.

## Evidence and limits

The former sites were not maintained, and I no longer have the original links or performance records. I do not present the articles as currently accessible or attach unverified ranking figures. The named topics are historical examples of my work, not links to recreated originals.

**What I take forward:** attention to reader interest and editorial voice, alongside the discipline to adapt content to its context.
