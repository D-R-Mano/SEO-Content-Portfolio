# Merge two tickets in RelayDesk

[Project overview](README.md) · [Why duplicate tickets need review](02-search-article.md)

*Fictional product procedure · Version 1.0*

Merge two tickets only after confirming that they concern the same issue. You must be an **admin or team lead**, both tickets must be **open**, and they must belong to the **same customer**.

## Before you begin

Choose a source ticket and a destination ticket. The destination is the conversation that will remain active. It will keep its existing owner and status.

**You cannot undo a merge.** Review both conversations and verify the two IDs before confirming. If you selected the wrong destination, cancel before the final step.

## Procedure

1. Open the source ticket.
2. Select **More → Merge ticket**.
3. Enter the destination ticket ID.
4. Review the source ID, destination ID, and customer shown on the confirmation screen.
5. Select **Confirm merge** only if the selection is correct.

## Verify the result

The source messages now appear in the destination conversation in chronological order. The source is closed and contains a reference to the destination. The destination retains its previous owner and status.

If those expected results are not visible, do not assume the merge succeeded. Contact support with the source ID, destination ID, and time of the attempt.

## Troubleshooting

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You are an agent | Ask an admin or team lead to review the tickets and perform the merge if appropriate |
| One ticket is closed | The pair is ineligible under this specification; ask your workspace admin how to handle it |
| Tickets belong to different customers | Do not merge them; the feature does not support this pair |
| All prerequisites are met but Merge is unavailable | Contact support with ticket IDs and the time of the attempt |
| You merged the wrong tickets | Contact your workspace admin; this product has no undo, and restoration is not promised |
| More duplicates appear later | A merge does not change routing or prevent new submissions; ask support to investigate recurrence |

Do not share passwords, access tokens, or full customer messages when requesting help in an unapproved channel.
