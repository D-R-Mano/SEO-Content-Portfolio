## My working approach

- **Begin with a question.** Separate the reader's immediate task from the business goal behind the content.
- **Choose the right format.** An article, procedure, and short answer serve different needs.
- **Keep claims traceable.** Distinguish product facts, editorial recommendations, and information that still needs checking.
- **Design for maintenance.** Give important content an owner, a source, and a reason to be reviewed.
- **Measure the next action.** Look beyond visits to whether readers can complete the task or find appropriate help.
